## Hi, I'm Mengru Zhang 👋

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at CNRS (ICGM, Montpellier), with a PhD in Chemical Engineering from University College London (UCL).

My research focuses on **computational chemistry, molecular simulation, and AI for materials discovery**, with particular interests in porous materials and molecular-level mechanisms in environmental and energy applications.

I work across multiple scales, combining:
- **DFT / TDDFT / AIMD**
- **Molecular Dynamics & Monte Carlo (MD, MC/GCMC)**
- **Enhanced sampling & free-energy calculations**
- **High-throughput computational screening**
- **Machine-learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) & GNNs**
- **HPC workflow development with Python and Bash**

I am particularly interested in understanding **structure–interaction–property relationships**, interfacial thermodynamics and kinetics, and using computational predictions to guide experimental material discovery.

### Tools & Methods
`Python` `PyTorch` `ASE` `CP2K` `VASP` `Gaussian` `GROMACS` `LAMMPS` `RASPA` `PLUMED` `MACE` `DeepMD-kit`
